About
A Love Racket.

An experiential event from The Risk of You, the vertical drama starring Ramsey Nouah. On 29 December, 500 singles meet over tennis, games and speed dating. Then 5,000 more arrive, and the night turns into a party.

Built for people who would rather meet someone across a court than across an app.
