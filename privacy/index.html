Privacy
What we collect and why.

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

Love Racket is an event produced by Boxonia Blueprint, taking place on 29 December 2026 at Queen's Park, Lagos. This site is operated by Mavedo Media on its behalf. For anything on this page, write to info@loveracket.co.

What we collect

Your email address and the player name you choose, when you log a score on the Love Racket game or sign up for updates. Your game scores and how long each game lasted. Standard analytics about your visit, such as the pages you opened, the device and browser you used, and the link that brought you here.

Why we collect it

To run the leaderboard and tell you where you stand, to contact the top five players about the prize, to send you updates about the event if you asked for them, and to understand which of our ads and posts are working.

Who sees it

We store this in Google Sheets and send email through Google and Zoho. We use Google Analytics and the Meta pixel, which means Google and Meta receive information about your visit. We do not sell your data and we do not share your email address with other companies.

Your player name is public

The name you choose shows on the leaderboard next to your score, where anyone visiting the site can see it. Your email address is never shown.

Stopping emails

Every email we send carries an unsubscribe link at the bottom, and it takes effect immediately.

Getting your data removed

Email info@loveracket.co and we will delete your record, including your leaderboard entry. We keep what is left for as long as the event and its follow up need it, and no longer.

Cookies

This site stores your player name and your best score in your own browser so the game remembers you. Google Analytics and the Meta pixel set their own cookies. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Children

Love Racket is an 18 and over event and this site is not intended for children.
